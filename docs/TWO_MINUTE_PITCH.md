# Blue Lagos — Two-Minute Pitch

*If you get only two minutes. Speak it, don't read it.*

---

**The problem.**
Lagos' riverine and coastal communities are planned for using Local Government averages. But an LGA average doesn't tell you whether a specific settlement can reach a clinic, or get a woman in obstructed labour out during a flood. Those communities are where a hazard turns into a fatality fastest, and they're the least visible in our data.

**What we built.**
Blue Lagos works at the level of the individual community. We surveyed 134 riverine communities — 93 with GPS locations — for population, sanitation, medical-evacuation status, flood exposure, erosion, and their own stated priority. We put those on the real Lagos State and Local Government boundaries, and alongside 539 health facilities, 292 jetties and landing points, and nearly 1,200 waterway segments from open mapping data, cleaned and checked.

**The evidence — one example.**
Take any located community. The platform shows the straight-line distance to the nearest mapped health facility. For 41 of our located communities that's over 5 kilometres; for 27 it's over 10 — and that's before you account for the fact that many can only be reached by water. It also flags communities that carry several access disadvantages at once, and lists every reason, so nothing is a black box.

**The decision value.**
You can test an intervention before spending on it. Drop a proposed health centre or jetty on the map, set a catchment radius, and immediately see which communities fall inside, how much known population, and how many of them are high-flood or hard to evacuate. Compare two candidate locations on the same transparent criteria.

**What we're careful about.**
Every distance is straight-line, not travel time — we say so. The facility layer is OpenStreetMap, not an official registry — we say so. Satellite flood data isn't connected yet — we say so. A missing number is shown as "Not recorded", never as zero.

**The ask.**
Two things. First, a decision on connecting Blue Lagos to LASWA, LASEMA and LASHMA data so the jetty and facility registries become official. Second, a nominated pilot corridor — Epe or the Badagry–Ojo axis — where we run this as the live planning view for the next quarter.
